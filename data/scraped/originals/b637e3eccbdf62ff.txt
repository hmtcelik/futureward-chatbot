If you are asked to provide Supplementary Documents, it will take longer.\nPassport Inspection: For overseas applicants, your local Taiwan embassy will inspect your passport (1 week). For domestic applicants, this step shall be skipped.\nSecondary Review: The National Immigration Agency (NIA) performs a final review of your application and prints your Gold Card (1-2 weeks).
